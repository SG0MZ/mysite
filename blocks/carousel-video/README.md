# carousel-video

Custom **carousel** block. Purpose: Slider of portrait (9:16) video poster cards; clicking a card plays the video in a fullscreen overlay.

## Authoring (Document Authoring)

Model: `standalone`

Single block table. Content: One row per slide: poster image | title, video link.

## Supported variations

No variations.

## Universal Editor fields

- Content fields derived from the block's decorate contract.

## Behavior

Slider of tall 9:16 video poster cards (about 3.5 visible) with play icon and white title overlay at the bottom; click opens a fullscreen video modal; prev/next arrows and progress bar; swipe/drag.

## Authoring notes

One row per slide: poster image | title plus a link to the video (mp4, YouTube or Vimeo). A slide without a video link renders as a plain image card.
